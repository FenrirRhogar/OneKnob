ONE KNOB  -  CHANGELOG

1.1.1
- Update notice: the version under the title shows "UPDATE x.y.z AVAILABLE" when a newer version is
  out. Click it for the download page, or to switch the check off. It asks GitHub at most once a day
  and sends nothing about you or your sessions.
- Linux Standalone: JACK support (PipeWire's JACK works too), for much lower latency than ALSA.
- macOS builds come from a newer build machine (macOS 15).

1.1.0
- New name: OneKnobToRuleThemAll is now One Knob. Old sessions open with the same settings.
Sound
- 8x oversampling: no more harsh aliasing at high drive, especially in the destroy mode.
- The output now stays as loud as the input across the whole knob, so you hear the colour, not
  just a louder signal (it used to get up to 11 dB louder halfway up the knob).
- Fixed: the destroy mode flipped the polarity of hot signals into a hard square edge.
- Fixed: the right channel could read past its filter state, which is undefined behaviour.
- Switching flavours crossfades instead of clicking.
- Host bypass parameter with a click-free crossfade.
Look
- Sharper artwork on HiDPI screens.
- The knob's ember glow grows with the drive and breathes with the audio; new level meter and
  an inked flavour selector.
- Fixed: the readout showed 0 % until you moved the knob.
Builds
- Native builds for Windows, macOS (universal, VST3 and AU) and Linux (VST3 and LV2), all with a
  Standalone app. Every build passes unit tests and pluginval at its strictest level; the AU also
  passes Apple's auval.
- Licence is now the GNU AGPL, version 3 or later.

1.0.0
- First release.
